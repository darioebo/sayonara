=======================================

===
A modern, minimal, flat app icon for "Sayonara", a presentation tool for video meetings (works on Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp). The icon depicts an abstract but clear representation of a "screen-sharing presentation layout": a clean window/screen with three distinct panels divided by visible thin gaps — ONE large panel on the left, and on the right TWO stacked panels (one above the other). This is the signature three-zone layout of the app: left large content area, right-top notes area, right-bottom content area.

Style: Apple-inspired, ultra-clean, flat vector look, gentle soft gradients, subtle depth, rounded corners, lots of negative space. No text, no letters, no words, no watermark. Centered composition. Squared (1:1) canvas for app icon.

Color palette: a calm, trust-inspiring set — soft white / off-white background, with a deep neutral charcoal or slate for the window outline, and a single muted accent color (e.g. soft indigo/blue or teal) used sparingly only for the "notes" panel to hint at the notes feature. Keep it minimal and elegant; avoid clutter and avoid multiple competing colors.

The image should feel premium, professional, calm and simple — like an Apple system app icon. No photorealism, no gradients that look cheap, no drop shadows that are too heavy.

IMPORTANT DIMENSIONS: Produce a SQUARE 1:1 icon at high resolution, at least 1024x1024 px, with the symbol centered and scaled to fill about 80% of the canvas with safe margins, no border frame. It will be resized automatically for PWA (512px and 192px) and used as favicon.
